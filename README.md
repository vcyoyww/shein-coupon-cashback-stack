## Why most SHEIN codes you find are dead on arrival

Coupon aggregators aren't lying to you on purpose. SHEIN rotates promo codes constantly, and a list that was accurate three weeks ago can be entirely expired today.

Both had expiry dates on them. Anyone reading that page in June was copy-pasting two dead codes. The same page's free-shipping code had no expiry at all, because free-shipping thresholds tend to be a store policy rather than a limited campaign.

Three things break codes more often than expiry does:

- **Region.** SHEIN runs separate storefronts per country. A code generated for shein.com/us doesn't necessarily work on the UK, Australian or Singapore site.
- **Customer status.** A lot of codes are first-order only. If your account has any order history, the field will reject it, sometimes silently.
- **Stacking rules.** Some SHEIN codes explicitly can't be combined with other offers. The field accepts one and drops the other without much explanation.

None of that is solvable by finding a better coupon list. It's solvable by checking the code in the place that has a reason to keep it current.

## What doesn't earn cashback at SHEIN

The exclusion list is short and worth knowing before you build a cart around it, because some of these are the exact items people buy in bulk.

- Gift cards, vouchers and store credit — purchases *and* redemptions
- Other loyalty programme rewards, unless stated otherwise
- Orders paid with promo codes that aren't featured on the platform
- Fees, taxes, service charges and shipping
- Returned, cancelled, refunded or exchanged purchases — partial returns get prorated, not paid in full

There's a nuance in the payment split. If part of an order is paid with a gift card or store credit, only the balance you actually pay by card or cash earns cashback. If that balance ends up at zero, there's no cashback at all.

SHEIN also watches for unusual buying patterns. Bulk buying and repetitive purchases of the same products can be flagged for review, and SHEIN reserves the right to cancel those transactions. Normal shopping isn't the target — the point is that buying twenty of one item through a cashback link is a good way to get the order investigated.
